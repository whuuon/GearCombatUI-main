# Figma asset provenance

Source design: [Figma file](https://www.figma.com/design/wMtwWaYIiKUchUdq5YdTm5?node-id=3761-2), file key `wMtwWaYIiKUchUdq5YdTm5`.

These assets were exported read-only. Exporting did not modify the Figma canvas. See `sources.json` for each stable asset's node ID, source link, dimensions, export method, file size, and SHA-256 digest. Temporary download URLs and session identifiers are not included.

Full component SVGs are retained as exported. Their Unity PNG partners were rasterized directly from those vectors with resvg at 4x. Export bounds may include strokes and effects beyond a node's nominal dimensions. No new artwork was generated for these assets.

`GearFrozen_Full.png`, `StonePhase1_Full.png`, and `PeanutDrone2_Full.png` were rendered by Figma at scale 4 from their original mixed bitmap/vector components. They are not SVG rasterizations. The original downloaded bitmap image fills are preserved separately as `GearFrozen.png`, `StonePhase1.png`, and `PeanutDrone2.png` at their original resolutions.

`BannerSource.png` is the original 869x1884 bitmap image fill of Figma banner node `3798:3832`. That source image is itself a full-screen screenshot. The Unity banner reproduces the Figma layer's clipped image fill using a UV crop of the preserved full bitmap. It is not an independent vector banner or a newly drawn banner.

This manifest covers stable full component exports and the four named original bitmap sources. Intermediate layer downloads and tutorial/slide reference crops are not part of its asset inventory.
